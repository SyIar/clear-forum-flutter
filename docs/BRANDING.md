Display name: `simpcity ultimate`. The bundle identifier and native channel remain unchanged for update compatibility. This is an independent personal reader.

## Final generation prompt

Create a production-ready square iPhone app icon for an independent forum reader named simpcity ultimate. Use case: logo-brand. Brand reference observed: the forum favicon has a bright turquoise upper area with white tall condensed SIMP lettering, and a near-black lower area with turquoise CITY lettering. Produce a NEW polished square interpretation in the SAME palette: turquoise #40B9C8, near-black #080D10, white #FFFFFF. Full-bleed 1024 by 1024 square, fully opaque, no pre-rounded outer corners and no white outside margin. Two clean horizontal color fields, turquoise on top and near-black below. Center two carefully aligned rows of highly legible tall condensed geometric lettering: exact text 'SIMP' above and 'CITY' below. White SIMP on turquoise; turquoise CITY on black. The full wordmark occupies roughly the central 72 percent with a comfortable icon-mask safe area. Refined slightly softened letter corners, crisp flat design, balanced spacing, strong small-size readability. No photos, characters, objects, gradients, tiny captions, badges, watermarks or mockup device. Deliver the icon alone.
